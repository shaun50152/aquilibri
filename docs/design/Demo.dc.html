Presenter's cheat sheet

  1. Open on the portal. Submit with the defaults. The button spends 1.5 seconds loading, then the job drops into the list.
  2. Switch to the operator. Point at the white node on the ring. That is JOB-014 on Alpha.
  3. Declare the cap. Emergency mode, Alpha, 300 L/h, 20:00 to 02:00. Hit Preview.
  4. Let the solver breathe. The scan rings buy you two seconds to say what the solver does.
  5. Read the explanation, then the table. The sentence is Gemini. The numbers are the solver.
  6. Apply. Watch the droplet land. Switch back to the portal, then advance the clock to completion.
Fallback if the backend stalls: the design uses clearly labeled sample data in an explicit visual-demo mode, never silently.
